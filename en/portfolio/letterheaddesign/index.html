The key principle behind all of these letterhead examples is balance. A letterhead should reflect your brand personality without taking attention away from the letter text, registration number, or date. It should never feel so busy that the main content gets lost. I also recommend taking a look at my business card design portfolio to see how the same visual identity can be carried across other brand materials.

In a company letterhead design, every graphic element is positioned with the document's intended use in mind. An accounting or law firm, for example, typically benefits from a clean, structured layout with a more formal feel. A creative studio or design agency has more room to experiment with distinctive forms, as long as the document remains practical to use.

When I work on an office letterhead design, I pay close attention to printing and typography details. Margins that are too narrow or a logo that takes up too much space can become a problem when longer documents need to be prepared. That is why I establish practical margins and type sizes from the beginning, so the finished professional letterhead remains both polished and easy to use.

The real test of a successful letterhead design is not how it looks in a mockup. A professional letterhead should still print clearly on a standard black-and-white office printer, and its PDF version should remain comfortable to read on a smartphone.

My approach to company letterhead design is systematic from the outset. A letterhead should not feel like a standalone piece; it needs to work naturally with the rest of your stationery. When your corporate colors, typography, and visual structure are coordinated properly, your official documents feel consistent, polished, and unmistakably part of the same brand.

This matters even more in office letterhead design because the template may be used hundreds of times across different documents. The layout therefore needs to leave enough room for employees or administrative staff to add tables, addresses, or longer sections of text without having to work around the design.
