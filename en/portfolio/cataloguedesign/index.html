The Catalog Design Process

Every catalog design project follows a clear process. I first learn about the business and understand the project's requirements, then review the content and structure before moving into design, final preparation, and file delivery. The goal is a polished visual result that is also ready for practical use by the brand.

First Understanding the Business

Understanding the Business

Understanding the nature of the business, the products or services it offers, the target audience, and the role the catalog is expected to play in presenting the brand, promoting products, or supporting the sales process.

Second Reviewing the Content

Reviewing the Content

Reviewing copy, images, product specifications, and company information, while organizing the material and defining the priority of each section so the page structure is clear from the beginning.

Third Developing the Structure

Developing the Structure

Defining the visual direction, page grid, and way images and information will be presented, then building a structure that can be applied consistently throughout the catalog.

Fourth Final Design

Final Design

Designing the complete set of pages and refining typography, images, colors, and visual details while ensuring that every section aligns with the brand identity and the overall catalog structure.

Fifth File Delivery

File Delivery

Preparing and delivering the final files for print or digital use, according to the catalog's purpose and the project requirements established from the beginning.
